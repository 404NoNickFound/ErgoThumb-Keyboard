ErgoThumb is a privacy-first keyboard designed for thumbs.

It uses two 3x3 grids with eight-direction swipe letters, a suggestion strip,
on-device learning, an optional spelling corrector, clipboard history,
language switching, cursor control, and deep theming.

Privacy:
- No Android permissions.
- No network code, analytics, or trackers.
- Learning data stays in private app storage and is removed on uninstall.

The project is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later. Source code, roadmap, and
privacy policy are available in the repository.
